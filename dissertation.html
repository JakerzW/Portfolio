Description

Overview & Research Goals

Completed a research-based dissertation project involving video games developed for mobile. The study focused on validating the positive cognitive effects of video games on the physical reaction-time of players.

Key Details & Implementations

  • Programmed a series of reaction-time testing games in Unity (C#).
  • Gathered gameplay metrics from dozens of test players, recording accuracy and latency.
  • Authored a comprehensive analysis paper evaluating the correlation between video games and physical reaction times.
  • Achieved a grade of 75% for the final dissertation thesis and code implementation.
